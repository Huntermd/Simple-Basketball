PIXEL COURTS - Basketball Court Backgrounds & Tiles
by Eric Lamb (ericlambdev)
Made to match the Pixel Baller sprite pack (80x140 players).

WHAT'S INSIDE
- scenes/  3 full side-view courts, 640x360 (1x) and 1280x720 (2x)
    gym     indoor arena: wood floor, crowd, banners, scoreboard
    street  night city court: asphalt, chain-link fence, skyline
    desert  sunset park court: concrete, mountains, saguaros
- layers/  each scene split into separate layers for parallax
- props/   hoops (indoor + street, left and right), scoreboard, bench,
           ball (16x16), saguaro
- tiles/   seamless 32x32 floor tiles: wood, asphalt, concrete

SCALE
The floor line sits at y=300 in every scene. Stand Pixel Baller
sprites with their feet on y=300. Rims are at a realistic 10 ft
for 80x140 players. Scale with nearest-neighbor filtering.
